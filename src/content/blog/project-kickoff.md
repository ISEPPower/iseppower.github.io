## A starting point

Demand Response Advisor for Smart Grids is a university group project by Equipa 10, developed in the context of the Master's Degree in Artificial Intelligence at ISEP.

Our goal is to explore an intelligent advisory system that helps energy consumers or prosumers make informed demand response decisions. The proposed system will connect an energy context to relevant knowledge and explain a suggested action.

## What we intend to explore

Possible scenarios include shifting flexible loads, avoiding peak consumption periods, responding to electricity price signals and adapting consumption to grid conditions. These are examples for scoping the project, not implemented capabilities or measured results.

Knowledge engineering is central to the proposed approach. We intend to document how domain knowledge is acquired, how it is represented and how reasoning could turn it into a recommendation. Sources, rules, tools and evaluation criteria remain to be defined.

## Why this blog exists

Over approximately six weeks, this journal will record project decisions, open questions and development progress. Planned topics include knowledge acquisition, knowledge representation, research, prototyping and, when available, evaluation results.

We will distinguish proposed work from completed work and include supporting evidence when reporting findings.

## Next steps

- Clarify the users, constraints and scope of the decision problem.
- Select a manageable set of demand response scenarios.
- Identify suitable knowledge sources and a representation approach.
- Agree on responsibilities and a development schedule.

The project is at its starting point. Technical content and team details will be added as the work develops.
